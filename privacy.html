Privacy Policy

Transparent, client-first data practices. Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. The Finculator Privacy Architecture

Finculator is engineered on a fundamental principle: your personal financial calculations belong exclusively to you. Unlike legacy financial portals and aggregator tools that monetize your loan queries or sell lead data to banks and insurance brokers, Finculator executes all financial calculations entirely on your client device using browser-native JavaScript engines.

2. Calculation Data & Local Storage

When you input your salary, home loan balances, mutual fund SIP allocations, or net worth figures into any of our 27 calculators:

  • Zero Server Transmission: Calculation parameters (loan amounts, interest rates, tenures, deductions) are never uploaded to our servers during regular calculator usage.
  • Local Persistence: If you modify calculator inputs, state is saved to your browser's localStorage or sessionStorage exclusively to preserve your working session across page reloads.
  • Complete Local Control: You can clear all cached numbers at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Reset Defaults" on any calculator.

3. Optional User Accounts & Authentication

Creating an account on Finculator is 100% optional. If you choose to register an account to sync personal portfolio goals:

  • Password Security: We enforce strict password complexity rules. Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are hashed using standard PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 computational rounds and unique 16-byte cryptographic salts.
  • Minimal Data Collection: We collect only your email address and encrypted authentication credentials.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your account credentials or email address to marketing agencies, loan brokers, or advertisers.
  • Account Deletion: You have the absolute right to terminate your account and delete all associated data at any time by contacting our security team.

4. FinBot AI Copilot & Proxy Queries

Finculator offers an optional AI financial assistant (FinBot). When you submit questions to FinBot:

  • Queries are processed through our hardened, rate-limited proxy with strict input length limits (1,000 characters).
  • Prompts are checked against a financial Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) knowledge base.
  • FinBot operates on an offline rule-based knowledge fallback when no external AI inference provider is configured.

5. Analytics & Advertising

Finculator displays zero third-party advertisements and runs zero cross-site tracking scripts. We do not use third-party behavioral profiling trackers.

6. Contact & Data Rights

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise data rights, please contact us at privacy@finculator.app.
